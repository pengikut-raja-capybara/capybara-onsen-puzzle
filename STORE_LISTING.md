### Full Description (max. 4000 characters)
```text
Unwind with an adorable Capybara! 🍊♨️

Capybara Onsen: Puzzle is a cozy, relaxing sliding puzzle game. Swipe to slide the Capybara past rocks, mud, wooden logs, river currents, thin ice, and whirlpool portals — collect Yuzu oranges, then sink into a warm Onsen hot spring.

No timers, no lives, no ads. Play anytime, at your own pace.

✨ KEY FEATURES

♨️ 75 Stages across 5 Themed Chapters
Each chapter brings a new board, palette, and mechanic:
1. Warm Onsen Pool — river stones & cozy mud
2. Yuzu Citrus Grove — push logs through a shady garden
3. Calm Bamboo River — water currents & bamboo gates
4. Snowy Onsen Peak — thin ice that cracks behind you
5. Lantern Night — whirlpool portals at the lantern festival
Every chapter ends with a handcrafted finale stage.

🛁 Interactive Onsen Sanctuary
Tap the Capybara on the home screen for floating hearts and soothing quotes. Decorate the pool with 6 items: Rubber Duckling, Bamboo Spout, Floating Yuzus, Sakura Branch, Warm Lantern, and a Baby Capybara soaking along.

🎩 Cute Accessories
Earn stars to unlock the Onsen Towel, Sakura Blossom, Bamboo Hat, and Royal Crown for your Capybara.

📅 Daily Stamp Calendar
Clear the daily puzzle and collect Hanko stamps on a monthly calendar. Missed a day? No worries — streaks never reset.

🍃 Zen Mode
Endless, light 5×5 puzzles with no move targets and no star ratings. Perfect for winding down before bed.

🏆 Endless Achievements
8 classic stamps plus 3 endless (∞) milestones — Devoted Daily Guest, Infinite Zen Spirit, and Onsen Legend — that keep leveling up forever. There's always a next goal!

🎮 Comfortable Controls
• Swipe directly on the board, or turn on the optional D-pad for one-handed play
• Unlimited Undo & a Hint button for the best next move
• Reduce Motion option for visual comfort

🎶 Soothing Atmosphere
A unique Kalimba soundtrack for every chapter, water & nature ambience, soft sound effects, and gentle haptics.

🔒 100% Offline & Private
No internet, no ads, no tracking, no account, and no device permissions. Your progress stays safely on your own device.

Ready for a soak? Download now and guide the Capybara to the Onsen! ♨️

Developed by: Pengikut Raja Capybara
Published by: Angsa Development Studio
```
